Over JSON-RPC (derived, UNVERIFIED on a live RPC): the keeper awaits its backend calls one after another, so a tick lasts ≈ wall + calls × RTT. With every market due every second (calls and wall fitted on the 50- and 200-market runs), one instance keeps this many markets observed every second: 657 at 5 ms, 365 at 10 ms, 123 at 30 ms, 69 at 50 ms, 26 at 100 ms. Targeted coverage makes a small share of markets due in any second (quiet markets every 30 s), so the covered-market capacity is several times these; above them the keeper needs concurrent calls or more instances (each with its own markets).
