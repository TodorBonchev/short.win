## Trading gate discovered on this devnet

The live devnet Pump `Global` seeds new curves with only **0.3 SOL virtual quote reserve**. This coin began with 1,073,000,000,000,000 virtual token units and 793,100,000,000,000 real token units. Buying every remaining real token would cost roughly 0.85 SOL net and leave only about 1.15 SOL virtual quote reserve before completion. Under ShortWin's Tier D 3% marginal-price-impact cap, the largest position there has only about 0.0169 SOL notional. The deployed `min_notional` is **0.1 SOL**, and the compiled minimum for any governance change is about **0.053 SOL**. Therefore this new devnet Pump coin cannot support a legal short even at full curve progress. This is an upstream devnet curve parameter mismatch, not evidence that the deposit or market instructions failed. No `open_short` or `close_short` transaction has been sent for this coin.

The older coin's curve retains a 30-SOL virtual quote reserve. Its mint has no mint or freeze authority, and its live curve is SOL-quoted, incomplete, non-Mayhem, non-cashback and has no editable creator-fee override. The proposed 4-million-token short would have roughly 0.115 SOL notional at current reserves and falls inside the 3% Tier D impact cap. Its external creator and mutable market activity remain live test dependencies. The first attempt to submit its BuyV3 packet returned HTTP 429 after a successful unsigned simulation, without a submission receipt; the buyer ATA and balance were unchanged. A later fresh packet was signed, sent, and confirmed at the receipt above. The BuyV3 helper now preserves a single signed transaction identity across bounded 429 retries and prints the signature before submission.

The shared public devnet RPC repeatedly returned HTTP 429 during the open preflight. The separate local Helius credential was secured at mode `0600`; a loopback-only, bounded relay now keeps its URL and key out of the keeper's arguments and logs. [Helius's published free limits](https://www.helius.dev/pricing) are 10 RPC requests/s, one `sendTransaction`/s and one million credits/month. The relay enforces eight requests/s, one send/s and a 10,000-request UTC-day cap. During the bounded trade smoke it forwarded 856 requests and 20 sends with zero local or upstream 429 responses. The keeper was restricted to **only** the deeper market.

## Unattended trading gate

This smoke does **not** establish continuous one-second oracle coverage. At a requested 400 ms tick interval, the keeper completed roughly nine ticks in 20 seconds during an empty-market sample, and the indexer recorded 11 observations by the end of the position smoke. The metrics reported no coverage misses or failed keeper transactions in that short window, but that is not evidence of sustained 1 Hz service. The externally created coin can also change independently. Keep the market paused for new opens until keeper throughput, cost budget and an alert route are verified for sustained active positions. The free Helius plan and current measured tick work do not yet support an unattended trading claim.
