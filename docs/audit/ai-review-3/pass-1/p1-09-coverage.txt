Account-review method
For every explicit field below I checked signer, owner, type, identity, PDA seeds/bump, mutability,
initialization, duplicate aliases, sysvar source, CPI program, and lamport/rent behavior.
The literal context constraints are retained below in field order, with handler dispositions following.
AccountLoader supplies owner/discriminator checks; Account supplies owner/discriminator/Borsh decoding.
InterfaceAccount checks the accepted owner set and decodes the token base state; a token_program relationship
needs a written constraint or the verified creation/transfer path. The pool-vault gap is a hardening LEAD.
Signer checks is_signer. Program<System> and Interface<TokenInterface> check program identity and executable status.
Canonical PDA bumps originate in init constraints, find_program_address, or fixed cache derivation.
Stored bumps cannot be chosen through an external instruction after initialization.
Distinct prefixes plus market/user/nonce/epoch fixed-width seeds separate accounts and prevent seed concatenation ambiguity.
Anchor 1.2.0 duplicate-mutable validation covers writable Account and InterfaceAccount fields, not all raw accounts.
Raw duplicates were checked against keys, discriminators, seed namespaces, and direct move from!=to checks.

CPI privilege results
- Native System, token, token-interface, ATA, and optional Memo program targets are typed or key-pinned.
  The optional Memo CPI uses the fixed memo ID and no account privileges.
- Venue programs are compile-time Pump, PumpSwap, Raydium v4, or CPMM IDs, with executable/key checks.
  Each trade CPI signs only the per-market trader PDA; user, keeper, position, and market-wide signing power is not forwarded.
  The pool-vault delegate is approved for the exact leg amount and checked after use; destinations and balance deltas are bound.
- init_uva forwards the outer creator signature to the fixed venue program solely as payer. It does not sign for trader.
- Anchor event CPI uses crate::ID, the fixed event-authority PDA and no financial accounts.
  Its generated receiver checks the event authority signature and key. The generated program field is not a caller-chosen CPI target.
- CPI-bearing helper structs (ExitAccounts, trader sides, Leg, ExtraContext) inherit caller constraints.
  The account map's native/Pinocchio label does not turn these Rust helpers into separately dispatched instructions.

Instruction-data results
lib.rs:74-324 defines the 30 exported Anchor handlers; it defines no custom fallback or native byte-dispatch handler.
Anchor checks discriminator prefixes, rejects unknown dispatch tags, and reports argument decode failures.
Borsh argument decoding may leave trailing bytes; reviewed handlers do not use those bytes as a second authority or account selection.
set_venue_extras bounds the decoded vector to four entries and each template's seed count/length.
An overlarge serialized vector can fail the caller's own decode, not bypass the signer/venue constraints.
The account lens traced numeric arguments to guards and typed math calls; it did not independently prove all math formulas.
