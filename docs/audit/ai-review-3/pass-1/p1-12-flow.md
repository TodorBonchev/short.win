title: The keeper retains readiness after external accounts close

code_smells: The keeper retains an unchanged readiness list without another account read; the program requires each recipient ATA to exist for every trade.
description: The keeper skips account checks after preparation; a closed recipient ATA may block automated exits, but recipient closure and operational recovery remain unverified.

violated_principle: The keeper should restore the external accounts that forced exits require after those accounts disappear.
proof: In-scope venue.rs:412–457 constructs the account list; graduation.rs:398–415 constructs idempotent ATA creation instructions with the keeper payer. In-scope pump_amm.rs:461–467 rejects an empty/non-token account; check_trade at 503–548 requires recipient ATAs to pass it. Out-of-bundle keeper.rs:2197–2213 reads missing accounts, but 2235–2237 skips that read for a cached list; 2240 and 2250 populate the cache. This establishes the stale-cache branch, not a complete in-scope exploit.
guards_and_limits: The account list changes when the chosen recipient or creator changes, which causes a fresh read. Anyone can recreate a recipient ATA externally. A keeper restart clears the process-local cache. The recipient is externally configured, so this is not evidence of an arbitrary unauthenticated caller's ability to close it. The external token-account authority/close-authority state and production operational recovery were not verified. The implementation that owns the cache, keeper.rs, is outside the frozen source bundle; retain as a context-supported lead, not an in-scope finding.
missing_proof: Establish that a selected recipient can and does close the required ATA without changing the key, and inspect deployed operational recovery before judging practical severity.
minimal_fix_candidate: Invalidate pamm_ready after account-validation failures, or periodically recheck the accounts before relying on cached readiness.
